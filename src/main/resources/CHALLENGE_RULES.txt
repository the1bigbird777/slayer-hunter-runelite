SLAYER + HUNTER MAIN — CHALLENGE RULES
Updated: 2026-10-11
Banking limits and Thieving streak bonuses remain undecided.

1. GENERAL RULES
• Slayer may be trained through assigned tasks; Hunter is unrestricted.
• Combat training and bosses are Slayer-task only, except approved activities: Pest Control, Barbarian Assault, quest-required fights and eligible off-task Combat Achievements.
• A Combat Achievement that can be completed on a Slayer task must be completed on-task.
• XP from quests, lamps, random events and achievement diaries is allowed in any skill.
• No direct player trading. Selling personally obtained items on the Grand Exchange is allowed.

2. SKILLS AND RESOURCES
• Slayer: fight monsters on assigned Slayer tasks.
• Hunter: completely unrestricted, at any time.
• Cooking: use food obtained through Hunter, Slayer or permitted purchases.
• Prayer: use bones obtained through Hunter or Slayer; don't buy bones.
• Farming: use Slayer-obtained seeds.
• Herblore: train using permitted Slayer/Farming ingredients.
• Runecrafting: train using pure essence from Slayer. Buying combat runes is permitted.
• Crafting and Smithing: use materials permitted by the challenge, primarily Slayer resources.
• Construction: use materials personally obtained from any permitted source.
• Woodcutting: use legitimately obtained axes with available durability charges.
• Mining: use legitimately obtained pickaxes with available durability charges.
• Firemaking: use creature-dropped logs or logs cut with permitted axes.
• Fletching: use permitted logs and other permitted resources.
• Fishing: acquire a permitted fishing tool before training; charge limits apply.
• Agility: earn five course laps per Slayer task; unused laps carry over, any unlocked course permitted. Travel shortcuts do not consume laps.
• Thieving: spend earned attempts; failures count as attempts.

6. AGILITY
• Completing a Slayer task earns five Agility laps.
• Unused laps carry forward.
• Laps may be spent at any unlocked Agility course.
• Shortcuts used for travel don't spend laps.

7. THIEVING
• Earn one attempt per 1,000 GP earned directly from Slayer monster drops.
• Earn one attempt for completing a Slayer task.
• GP gained through High Alchemy or sales does not count.
• Failed attempts consume an attempt; unused attempts carry forward.
• STREAK BONUS DECISION PENDING: Previously +10 at streak 10, +50 at 50, +100 at 100 (non-stacking). User may remove these bonuses. CURRENT PLUGIN LOGIC STILL AWARDS THEM until changed.

8. FOOD AND POTIONS
• Food and potions MAY be purchased.
• Food can alternatively come from Hunter and Slayer; potions can be made through permitted Herblore.

9. PURCHASES AND ITEM SOURCING
• Buying weapon/armour upgrades, ammunition and runes is allowed.
• For restricted tools and items available as Slayer creature drops, obtain them from those creatures rather than buying them.
• Do not trade directly with other players.
• You may sell personally obtained items freely through the GE.
• Ground spawns are permitted only where no creature/Hunter/other permitted source can supply the item.

10. QUESTS, DIARIES AND CLUES
• All quests are allowed. Get required items from creature drops when possible; otherwise an alternate source may be used. Quest-only items are permitted.
• Quest XP may be awarded to any skill.
• Achievement diaries of any tier may be completed when their requirements can be met under the normal restrictions. Diary rewards and XP lamps are permitted; diary activities do NOT grant a blanket exemption to training restrictions.
• All clue tiers may be completed at any time; rewards may be used or sold subject to existing combat/storage rules.

11. MINIGAMES AND COMBAT EXCEPTIONS
• Pest Control and Barbarian Assault are allowed for combat training and rewards.
• Quest-required fights are allowed.
• Off-task Combat Achievements are allowed only when the achievement cannot be done during a Slayer task.
• Skilling minigames, including Wintertodt, Tempoross and Guardians of the Rift, are not allowed for XP/rewards.

12. DEATH AND BANKING
• You may recover your own items after death by any method.
• Banking is limited; the exact whitelist is not yet final.
